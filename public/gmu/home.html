GM University and Signs IIIT Dharwad Research Park (IDRP) Memorandum of Understanding (MoU)

for Academics & Research.

We are delighted to announce that GM University, Davanagere — an emerging hub of academic excellence and innovation — has officially signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the IIIT Dharwad Research Park (IDRP) on 9th July 2025.

This strategic partnership aims to foster collaborative research and promote deep-tech innovation.
It will also provide incubation support for student and faculty-led startups, facilitate skilling programs to enhance employability, and promote faculty development initiatives aligned with industry needs.

Together, we are committed to shaping a future where knowledge translates into application and innovation drives meaningful real-world impact.
